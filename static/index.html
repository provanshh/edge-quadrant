How EdgeMind works

Think of it as a camera with a memory. It does not need the internet to remember or to answer questions.

1It watchesThe device looks at every frame of the video and spots objects like chairs, lamps or bottles.
2It remembersEach new thing is saved on the device with a picture and a short description.
3Ask anythingType what you're looking for in plain words. Answers come in milliseconds, even offline.
4It backs upWhen online, memories upload to the cloud. Offline changes wait in line and upload later.
5You decideIf HQ and the device change the same note, you pick which one to keep.
🔒PrivacyThings seen in private rooms (bathroom, bedroom) stay on the device only.
Try it: start the video → click Go offline → search “bar stools” (still works!) → click Try a conflict → click Go online → pick which note to keep.
